Resolution

Two ways to get a pack.

Curated · verified

Hand-built for popular domains — Backend Python, ML, Quant Trading, UX, Data Eng, Calculus. Faculty, syllabus, rubrics & tools are reviewed. Used directly when the intent matches.

Backend PythonMachine LearningUX DesignCalculus

Generated · always works

No match? The pack is generated from the intent at intake — drafted, validated, cached, and used. It improves as the learner progresses, and good ones get promoted.

"teach me falconry" → drafted pack → staffed

The flywheel

Generated packs that produce strong learner outcomes (high quality) get reviewed and promoted to curated. The library grows itself from real usage — coverage compounds.
