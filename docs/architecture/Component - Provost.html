The Provost is the optimizer.

When a faculty agent's reward falls, something has to change its policy. That's the Provost: it watches teaching outcomes, diagnoses why a teacher is failing the learner, and ships a rewritten methodology. It is the policy-update operator for every professor in the college.

role=policy-update engine input=faculty reward + traces output=new methodology doc not=weight updates
The update pipeline

Detect → diagnose → rewrite → ship → watch.

The Provost runs whenever a teacher's cumulative reward over a unit drops below threshold. It never touches model weights — it edits the methodology the policy is conditioned on.

1
DetectΣ reward for a unit falls below the bar, or a mid-term is failed.
2
DiagnoseRead the lesson traces & probe history to classify the failure mode.
3
Select interventionPick the fix from the intervention library that targets that mode.
4
Compose methodologyWrite a new versioned methodology doc — concrete, structured deltas.
5
ShipDispatch to the agent's Update Hook; bump its policy version.
6
Track recoveryWatch the next unit's reward; revert or iterate if it doesn't lift.

Why methodology, not weights

Fine-tuning a model per student is slow, costly and opaque. AULA keeps the LLM fixed and treats the methodology document as the editable policy. Updates are instant, legible, and reversible.

policy = LLM (fixed) + methodology (edited by Provost)

Diagnosis → intervention

The failure-mode catalogue.

The diagnoser maps an observed pattern to a root cause, and the library maps that cause to a concrete methodology change.

Observed patternDiagnosed causeIntervention applied
Fails probes right after readingNo practice bridgeInsert worked examples before probing
Strong early, collapses on examPacing too fastSlow the unit; add spaced revision
Disengages, attendance dropsWrong modality / too denseSplit blogs; add images & shorter steps
Confuses prerequisite conceptsMissing foundationBack-fill a prerequisite mini-unit
Right answers, wrong reasoningPattern-matching, not understandingAdd Socratic probes & "explain why"
