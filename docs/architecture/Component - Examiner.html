The Examiner is the referee.

Reward is only as good as its measurement. The Examiner sets the pass-bar, grades blind, and decides whether you advance or repeat. Its verdict becomes the scalar that rewards or penalizes the teacher — and the gate that wakes the Provost on failure.

role=assessment + pass-gate output=pass / fail + reward on fail=block + re-enroll principle=blind & calibrated
The pass-gate

One verdict, two cascades.

Every assessment resolves to a gate. Pass advances the learner and rewards the teacher; fail blocks progression, penalizes the teacher, and triggers a policy update.

On pass

Learner advances to the next unit. Teacher receives + reward sized to the assessment weight; grade rises.

advance · +reward · grade ↑

On fail (mid-term)

Learner does not pass and re-enrolls the unit — no penalty to them. Teacher receives full − reward; the Provost rewrites the method.

Integrity by design

Items vary every attempt, grading is blind to the teacher, and "explain your reasoning" probes catch pattern-matching. The Examiner is the one agent the faculty can't charm.
