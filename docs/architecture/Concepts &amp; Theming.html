Who it's for

The same college, dialed for the learner.

Purpose flexes with age and goal. A child's academy is pure play — no exams, no placement. A graduate's is outcome-driven and employer-facing. Faculty, tone, and the reward loop all retune; the career layer only switches on when it's wanted.

1
Young learnersPlay & curiosity. No exams, no placement — just wonder and streaks.
2
School & collegeReal subjects, trials and ranks. Mastery first; career layer optional.
3
Career switchersFast, targeted, market-driven. Showcase project front and centre.
4
Graduates & job-seekersPlacement-driven. Resume, company connect, real intros — the full outcome engine.
