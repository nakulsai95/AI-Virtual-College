Subsystem architecture · the planner

A mission becomes a semester.

The Principal decides who to hire; the Curriculum Builder decides what they teach and when. It decomposes your mission and the domain pack into a structured semester — subjects, modules, tasks, exams, on a calendar — and rebuilds it when you re-enroll.

input=mission + domain pack output=semester object grain=subject→module→task rebuilds=on re-enroll
The decomposition

The goal hierarchy, build-side.

Each level traces up to your mission — the same chain every agent reads for context.

1
Mission"Become job-ready in backend Python" — captured at intake.
2

3
ModulesEach subject → ordered units, each with its own kanban & exam.
4
TasksEach module → concrete tasks, some tool-backed (run in the sandbox).
5
ExamsGates attached at module & mid-semester boundaries.

It rebuilds, not just builds

On a failed gate, a pace change, or a Provost methodology update, the builder re-plans the affected modules — re-sequencing, slowing, or injecting a prerequisite — without scrapping the whole semester.

re-enroll · pace change · methodology update → partial rebuild
