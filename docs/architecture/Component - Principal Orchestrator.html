The Principal is the manager.

Where a faculty agent optimizes one subject, the Principal sits a level up — a hierarchical-RL manager. It reads your intent, hires the faculty, and hands each agent a sub-goal, a reward shape, and a budget. Its own reward is the only one that counts in the end: your mastery.

role=manager / orchestrator reward=your overall mastery sets=sub-goals · rewards · budgets governs=hire · pause · replace
The hierarchy

You set the mandate. It sets everyone else's.

Three tiers of control. The board (you) governs the Principal; the Principal governs the faculty — passing goals and rewards down, pulling state and outcomes up.

Goal & reward shaping

One mission, decomposed into aligned rewards.

The Principal's core trick: split your mission into sub-goals and give each agent a reward that, when maximized, advances the whole. Example mission — "Become job-ready in backend Python."

Cost governance

Every agent has a model and a cap.

The Principal picks the LLM per role and a monthly token budget. Hit the cap and the agent pauses — no runaway spend, the cost stays the learner's to see and govern.

Governance · the board

The Principal answers to you.

Autonomy is granted, not assumed. The Principal can act on the faculty, but it can't act on the college's shape without your sign-off.

approve
Hires & the planNo faculty goes live until you approve the proposed college.
override
StrategySwap a subject, change the pace, redefine the goal — anytime.
pause
Any agentFreeze a teacher or the whole college on demand.
reassign
Roles & budgetsMove work, raise or cut an agent's spend cap.
replace
A failing teacherRetire a professor whose grade won't recover; hire a new one.

Who can do what

The Principal acts downward on faculty freely — pause, reassign, push a methodology update. It acts outward on the college's shape only with your approval.

Principal → faculty: autonomous.
Principal → college shape: board-gated.
