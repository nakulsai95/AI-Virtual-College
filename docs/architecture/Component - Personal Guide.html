Component architecture · 05 of n

The Guide is the router.

You never have to know which professor owns a topic. The Personal Guide is the one agent you always talk to: it classifies any doubt, decides whether to answer it itself or hand it to the right teacher, and makes sure the answer lands on your board as a lesson — never lost in chat.

role=student-facing concierge input=any doubt, any phrasing output=routed answer + lesson card reward=fast, correct routing
The routing pipeline

Doubt in. Right teacher out.

The Guide is a dispatcher policy. Its reward is resolution speed × correctness — misroutes and unresolved doubts cost it.

1
ReceiveA doubt in natural language, any time, any subject.
2
ClassifyMap it to a subject & the owning professor — or "general".
3
DecideAnswer it directly if light; route if it needs the specialist.
4
Hand offPass full context to the professor so they don't re-ask.
5
PersistThe answer becomes a lesson card on the student kanban.
6
ConfirmCheck the doubt is resolved; reopen or escalate if not.

Self-answer vs. route

The Guide answers logistics, recaps and pointers itself. Anything that teaches a concept is routed to the owning professor — so teaching credit (and reward) lands on the right agent.

light → Guide answers · conceptual → professor teaches
