The Counselor is the guardian.

A college that only optimizes mastery will burn its students out. The Counselor is the counterweight: it watches for frustration, fatigue and stalling, and it can slow the whole machine down. Its reward is the one thing no teacher's is — your sustained engagement.

role=wellbeing & pacing watches=burnout signals reward=sustained momentum can=override the pressure
Detection signals

What burnout looks like in the data.

The Counselor fuses behavioural and sentiment signals into a single risk read — acting before the learner quits.

The intervention ladder

The gentlest fix that works.

The Counselor climbs only as far as it must — most risk resolves at a nudge.

1
NoticeRisk score crosses a soft threshold.
2
NudgeAn encouraging, specific message — celebrate a win, reframe a setback.
3
Adjust paceAsk the Principal to slow the schedule or lighten the load.
4
Recommend a breakProtect a rest day; pause streak penalties.
5
EscalatePersistent risk → flag to the Principal & surface to the learner.

The counterweight

Faculty are rewarded for your mastery; the rival pushes you; the leaderboard ranks you. The Counselor is the only agent rewarded for your sustainability — and it can overrule the pressure when it's too much.

mastery ⟂ wellbeing — the Counselor holds the balance
